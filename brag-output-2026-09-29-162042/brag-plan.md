# Orbit launch video plan (hype cut)

## What it is
Orbit is a personal assistant for everyday life: email, calendar, bills, bookings, shop orders and
health in one app, with a daily briefing and plain-English answers that show their sources.

- **For:** busy adults running a job, a household and a social life from one phone.
- **Sets it apart:** one briefing across everything; ask a question and get an answer with its sources.
- **Visual hook:** a storm of real sample-data notifications piling up around fast-cycling words,
  then "One phone. Too many moving parts." before everything is pulled into the Orbit mark.
- **Real UI shown:** the app's own Today, Ask Orbit (question typed and answered), Money bills,
  Plans itinerary, then Inbox, Calendar, Tasks, Business, Health and Deliveries. All sample data,
  labelled "Demo data".
- **Tone:** hype. Big serif type slamming in on the beat, a build and a hard drop on the logo,
  8th-note cuts in the montage. Kept to the brand's voice: no exclamation marks, no invented claims.

## Format
Landscape 1920x1080, 30fps, 22.5s. Music at 120 BPM (0.5s per beat), A minor (Am, F, C, G).
Every cut lands on a beat or an eighth note.
