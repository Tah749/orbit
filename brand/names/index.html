16 names for a calmer life assistant

These are alternative names for Orbit, the privacy-first assistant that pulls email, calendar, bills, investments, travel, fitness and tasks into one daily view. The list mixes invented words, Latin, nautical and old-trade words, and a few bolder picks. Each name has a geometric mark, shown in colour, mono and light versions, and at favicon size.

Top 3 picks

1

The meaning is the product: a window that shows your whole life at once. It is a real word that sounds premium and is easy to say, and a quick search found no software using it. The arched-window mark stays readable at 16px and belongs to no one else.

 2

The most ownable name here. It is invented, two syllables, warm rather than techy, and works for families as well as professionals. A search found no products using it. The T-over-V monogram is the strongest favicon in the set.

 3

It names the feeling rather than the feature: calm after the chaos of too many apps. It is an invented word but you can still tell it comes from "halcyon". The wave-to-flat-line mark tells the story on its own. There are a few small users of the name (a travel app, a healthcare marketing site), so check the name early.

Reeve has the best meaning of the lot (a steward who runs the household's affairs), but several AI startups already use the name, so it is ranked lower. None of these names has been trademark-cleared. The "Noticed" notes come from a quick web search only.
