# Orbit: alternative name candidates

This is an exploration only. Nothing here is trademark-cleared, and domains were not checked for availability. The notes in the "Noticed" column come from a quick web search.

## Top 3

1. **Oriel**: The meaning is the product: a window that shows your whole life at once. It is a real word that sounds premium and is easy to say, and a quick search found no software using it. The arched-window mark stays readable at 16px and belongs to no one else.
2. **Tuvo**: The most ownable name here. It is invented, two syllables, warm rather than techy, and works for families as well as professionals. A search found no products using it. The T-over-V monogram is the strongest favicon in the set.
3. **Halcy**: It names the feeling rather than the feature: calm after the chaos of too many apps. It is an invented word but you can still tell it comes from "halcyon". The wave-to-flat-line mark tells the story on its own. There are a few small users of the name (a travel app, a healthcare marketing site), so check the name early.

Reeve is the honourable mention. Its meaning is the closest fit, but the name is crowded with AI startups.
