Orbit, 16 logo concepts

Wordmarks, lettermarks and abstract symbols for Orbit, the calm, private AI assistant for your whole life. The palette is neutral near-black and off-white with a single accent (#FF4D7A), so the forms are what you judge. Every mark also works in one colour.

Top 3

1Eccentric O

It's the most ownable and scalable mark here. It's a single solid shape, so it's crisp at 16px and strong in an icon grid. It says 'orbit' through physics, not a space cliché. It works without the accent, so the brand isn't tied to one colour.

See concept →

2rb Link

It puts the product idea (connecting separate things) into the letterforms themselves. It gives a custom wordmark nobody else has and a two-letter monogram for the icon. It's quiet enough for a calm, privacy-first brand.

See concept →

3Open O

It's the clearest story in one glance: a loop left open for you to close, which says control, not surveillance. It's minimal and friendly, and the accent dot gives a natural hook for motion (the dot settling into the gap) at launch.

See concept →
