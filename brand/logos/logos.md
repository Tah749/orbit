## Top 3

1. **Eccentric O** (`eccentric-o`): It's the most ownable and scalable mark here. It's a single solid shape, so it's crisp at 16px and strong in an icon grid. It says 'orbit' through physics, not a space cliché. It works without the accent, so the brand isn't tied to one colour.
2. **rb Link** (`rb-link`): It puts the product idea (connecting separate things) into the letterforms themselves. It gives a custom wordmark nobody else has and a two-letter monogram for the icon. It's quiet enough for a calm, privacy-first brand.
3. **Open O** (`open-o`): It's the clearest story in one glance: a loop left open for you to close, which says control, not surveillance. It's minimal and friendly, and the accent dot gives a natural hook for motion (the dot settling into the gap) at launch.

## Notes
- All resemblance flags are informal visual judgements, not trademark searches. Run a proper clearance before choosing a mark.
- Wordmark text is set in Geist / Inter with a system-ui fallback. For production, outline the chosen wordmark to paths.
