# Orbit launch video plan

## What it is
Orbit is an AI assistant that brings your email, calendar, bills, bookings, investments,
fitness and tasks into one place and tells you what matters today.

- **For:** busy people whose life is scattered across a dozen apps.
- **Sets it apart:** one daily briefing across everything; answers that cite their sources;
  it asks before acting.
- **Best claim (the site's own):** "Your life, finally connected."
- **Visual hook:** the app chips from the site's "Your life is everywhere" section drift
  apart, then collapse into the Orbit orb.
- **Real UI shown:** the hero dashboard, the Life Inbox, the "Just ask Orbit" chat with
  source cards, the "Ask before acting" setting, and the waitlist button with its real
  success state.
- **Tone:** `polished`. Serious, calm, premium; soft transitions and long holds.
- **Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.

## Angle
Chaos into calm. Your life is in seven apps; Orbit pulls it into one place, then earns
trust by showing its sources and asking before it acts.

## Format
Landscape 1920×1080, 30fps, 22.5s. Music at 100 BPM (0.6s per beat); every cut lands on a beat.

**Poster / frame 0:** Scene 2 settled (headline and dashboard), about 6.9s.

## Sound
Composed as one piece in D major at 100 BPM: warm pad, sub bass with gentle sidechain, soft
kick, light shaker. UI effects are tuned notes from the same key (D major pentatonic) and sit under the music.
Nothing bright or spiky; everything is low-passed and runs through a shared reverb.
